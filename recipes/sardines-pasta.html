Description

Spanish Sardines Pasta is a light, savory dish featuring freshly cooked pasta tossed in a garlic and olive oil (aglio e olio) infusion

Ingredients

  • Spanish Sardines
  • Olives
  • Garlic
  • Tomatoes
  • Mushrooms
  • Olive Oil
  • Pasta Noodles

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta according to package instructions until al dente (usually 8–10 minutes). Reserve ½ cup of pasta water, then drain the noodles and set them aside.
  2. Crisp the garlic: Heat the 2 tablespoons of olive oil and the reserved oil from the sardine bottle in a large pan over low heat. Add the sliced garlic and sauté until it turns light golden brown and fragrant. Watch it closely so it does not burn.
  3. Sauté the vegetables: Toss in the diced tomatoes and sliced mushrooms. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until the mushrooms soften and the tomatoes start to break down.
  4. Add the highlights: Stir in the sliced olives and the flaked Spanish sardines. Gently toss for about 1 minute just to heat the fish through without breaking it up completely. Season with a pinch of salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes if you want a kick.
  5. Combine everything: Add the drained pasta directly into the pan. Pour in a splash of the reserved pasta water to help create a glossy sauce that coats the noodles. Toss everything together over low heat for 1 minute.
  6. Serve: Transfer to plates and top generously with grated parmesan cheese and fresh parsley.
